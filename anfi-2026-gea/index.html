Digital twins of students and the Generative-Evaluative Agreement

A 30-minute workshop on using simulated students to calibrate assessment items and assessment bots, and on why the scorer and the simulator can disagree.

  1. Intro~7 minTwins, four uses, the closed loop, adaptive drift, and the fixes.
  2. Demo~8 minReal simulated cohort: one student, the whole class, skill by skill.
  3. Activity~15 minIn pairs with a free web chatbot: make five twins, catch the drift, try one fix. Any subject.
